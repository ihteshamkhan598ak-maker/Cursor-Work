## Step 3 — What this means for the doctor-portable app

### Specialty dropdown (use the 43 normalized specialties above)

Build the app's specialty list from the 43 normalized specialties, not from raw source spellings. Keep "Paediatric/Pediatric" and "Orthopedics/Orthopedic Surgery" merged so one specialty does not appear twice.

### Multi-affiliation proof (the portable-doctor idea matches reality)

4 confirmed doctors practise at more than one facility, including 2 cross-district (Charsadda + Peshawar). This confirms the app's core design: the doctor owns the profile, facilities are only affiliations, and changing affiliation must not delete history. Within Charsadda district alone the count is 0 — multi-affiliation is a Peshawar/cross-district pattern in this data, not a Charsadda pattern.

### Fields usually published vs usually missing (what the app must ask doctors to fill in)

- **Usually published:** doctor name, specialty, facility affiliation, city (directories and official schedules).
- **Often published (private directories only):** qualifications (mixed — frequently blank on official pages), consultation fee (private doctors only; figures conflict across directories, e.g. NWGH's Dr. Samia Zahid Aman at Rs. 1,500 on Healthwire vs Rs. 4,000 in an older oladoc snippet), timings (private directories; government rows carry OPD days only, not appointment times).
- **Usually missing:** named doctors at government hospitals — Lady Reading Hospital, Khyber Teaching Hospital, Peshawar Institute of Cardiology (full list), IRNUM, THQ Shabqadar and THQ Tangi publish departments/OPD schedules, not named consultant lists. Qualifications on official pages (PGH doctors page, HMC OPD schedule give mostly no degrees). Any fee for government OPD. Individual appointment times anywhere except private directory slots.
- **App consequence:** the app cannot be seeded from official hospital sites for government doctors — those rosters are not public. Onboarding must ask each doctor to fill in qualifications, fee, timings, and affiliations themselves, and directory fees must be phone-verified before being shown to patients.

## Gaps — what could not be verified (do not fill these by guessing)

### Peshawar gaps

- No public named doctor list found for Lady Reading Hospital, Khyber Teaching Hospital, Peshawar Institute of Cardiology (full list) or IRNUM — only departments/OPD schedules. Department names at those hospitals must NOT be treated as doctor names.
- No full official doctor list found for RMI or NWGH — RMI's official consultants page surfaced only one full profile; NWGH's official site shows departments, not names. Healthwire states 59 doctors at NWGH but only shows the first 5; the rest were not copied.
- Timings and fees are almost never published on official hospital pages — only a minority of directory rows carry them.
- Qualifications frequently blank on official pages (PGH, HMC). Blank means "not published on the page read", not "unqualified".
- Fee conflicts across directories must be re-checked before use (see Dr. Samia Zahid Aman example above).
- Kuwait Teaching Hospital type is ambiguous: one secondary source calls it public, its own description calls it a charity hospital. Recorded as Private (charitable teaching) with this caveat; do not reclassify as government without a primary source.
- Excluded: LMC Hospital (its address is Swabi, not Peshawar); an uncorroborated Graana "Prime Hospital faculty" list; small Peshawar clinics/centres (outside major-hospital scope); one oladoc NWGH page that returned a Cloudflare sign-in block (not used).
- HMC's official pages give no street address; its Phase 3 Hayatabad area comes from a directory listing.

### Charsadda gaps

- No official facility website or "Our Doctors" page was found for ANY Charsadda facility — all Charsadda rows are directory/secondary/news sources, stated as such. The KP Health site publishes no Charsadda doctor list.
- Government hospitals publish almost no named doctors: THQ Shabqadar names none, THQ Tangi only a surgeon via a news report, DHQ Charsadda's directory listing names only 2–3 doctors despite its broad department list.
- Dr. Ashfaq Utmanzay affiliation conflict: an older Marham page places him at DHQ Hospital; his current profile shows his own Family Clinic with DHQ only under past experience. Re-check before use. His qualifications also vary between pages.
- Timings/fees almost never published for government hospitals; private-clinic fees are directory figures to phone-confirm before quoting.
- Small-clinic specialty tags without names (Abid Mian Plaza dermatology, Afghan Medicos dermatology, Al Hafeez Surgical eye, Sarhad eye) produced no doctor rows — tags are not doctors.
- Facilities with no usable named-doctor list: Al Hafeez Medical Center, Abdal Medical Complex, New City Medical Centre (Shabqadar), Gul Baba Plaza Hospital; Shah Medical Complex is stated to have 5 enlisted doctors but only 1 is named on the pages read.
- Oladoc "Charsadda" city pages overstate local availability — they mostly list video-consultation doctors based elsewhere; only doctors tied to a Charsadda facility address were included. Do not reuse oladoc city-page lists as a Charsadda doctor list without that facility check.
- Name-spelling variants: "Dr. Makeel Shah" also spelled "Makaeel" in the same report; "Al Khidmat" vs "Al-Khidmat"; "Sudais" vs "SUDAIS".
- Several Charsadda addresses are plus-codes or landmarks only; no street-level address was found for THQ Tangi, THQ Shabqadar or DMRC.
- Private-facility department lists in Charsadda are mostly inferred from their listed doctors, not from a published department list — do not present them as official rosters.
